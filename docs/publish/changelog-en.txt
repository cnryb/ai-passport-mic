First release: turn AI Passport into a pocket wireless microphone.

- Tap OK to start and stop talking; your voice goes straight into your meeting app's mic input
- The device screen shows battery, connection state and what each of the three buttons does;
  hold UP/DOWN to turn the screen on or off
- UP and DOWN can be mapped to any shortcut, so you can raise a hand or switch slides without
  reaching for the keyboard
- The companion app can verify your audio driver in one tap: it plays a test tone and reads it
  back from the input side, and tells you whether the driver is missing or microphone permission
  was denied
