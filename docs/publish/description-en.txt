Turn AI Passport into a pocket wireless microphone. In meetings, recordings or livestreams the
device is your mic: tap OK to start talking, tap again to stop, and your voice goes straight into
your meeting app's microphone input. No headset cable, no leaning into the laptop. UP and DOWN
can be remapped to any shortcut — raise a hand, switch slides, mute.

Requires a Mac and a small companion desktop app (one right-click Open the first time).

The device screen shows battery, connection state and what each button does; it dims after
30 seconds of inactivity to save power.

Good for remote meetings, online classes, quick podcast takes and livestream audio.
